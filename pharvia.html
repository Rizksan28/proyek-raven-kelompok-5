01 Project Overview

Reimagining Pharmaceutical Waste Management.

PHARVIA is a digital pharmaceutical take-back ecosystem designed to make unused and expired medicine returns safer, easier, and more transparent.

The platform connects consumers, pharmacies, pharmaceutical companies, and licensed waste management partners through digital medicine scanning, collection points, reward incentives, smart collection coordination, and end-to-end traceability.

02 The Challenge

The Problem We Set Out to Solve.

課題
  1. 01

    Improper disposal

    Leftover household medicines still end up in ordinary household waste.

  2. 02

    Environmental contamination

    Drug residues can enter soil and water, increasing ecological risk.

  3. 03

    Limited return channels

    Convenient, trusted places to hand back unused medicines are hard to find.

  4. 04

    No post-consumer traceability

    Once medicines leave the pharmacy, their end-of-life journey is difficult to track.

  5. 05

    Low awareness

    Many people are unaware of how to dispose of medicines responsibly — or why it matters.

03 Our Solution

One Connected Ecosystem. Measurable Impact.

PHARVIA is proposed as an integrated digital ecosystem: one flow that takes a medicine from the user's cabinet to a verified, licensed disposal — with every step recorded.

  • 01

    Pharvia Scan

    Medicine scanning and return guidance.

    Scan a medicine, check its expiry date, and receive guidance on how to return it.

  • 02

    Pharvia Point

    Nearby pharmacy and healthcare collection points.

    Connects users with the nearest pharmacies and healthcare facilities acting as return points.

  • 03

    EcoPoint

    Reward incentives for responsible medicine returns.

    Rewards users who return medicines safely and responsibly, encouraging repeat participation.

  • 04

    Smart Collect & Traceability

    Collection coordination and pharmaceutical waste tracking.

    Records each return digitally, optimises pickup schedules and routes, and tracks medicines through to disposal.

Proposed features shown as UI mockups from the competition entry — not a production deployment.

05 Business Model & Impact

Beyond an Application. Building an Ecosystem.

A proposed B2B2C model: businesses fund and host the network, while consumers drive participation.

  1. 01

    Pharmaceutical take-back partnerships

    Corporate take-back programmes give pharmaceutical companies a structured channel for their products' end of life.

  2. 02

    Pharmacy collection networks

    Pharmacies host drop boxes and QR returns prepared by PHARVIA, gaining foot traffic and customer loyalty in return.

  3. 03

    Sustainability reporting

    An ESG & Impact Dashboard turns return data into measurable sustainability reporting for partners.

  4. 04

    End-to-end traceability

    Every stage is recorded, and disposal is handled only by licensed waste-management partners.

  5. 05

    Reward-driven participation

    EcoPoint incentives, personal reminders, and education help turn responsible disposal into a habit.
